## 2. Root causes, ranked

1. **The core-cache exclusion set names files by hand and got two wrong / one missing** (`core_cache.py:264-287`): `"drain_state.json"` ≠ `dispatch_delivery.DRAIN_STATE_FILENAME` (`"dispatch_delivery_drain.json"`), and `serve_socket.SOCKET_OWNER_FILENAME`/`serve_socket.lock` are absent. Both files are rewritten by every serve boot (owner) or every 60 s (drain), neither is a build input, and together they guarantee a fingerprint miss on every same-commit boot. Certain; measured; the fix is mechanical.
2. **The build perturbs its own inputs and the key is pre-build by design**: opening the SessionDB in WAL mode creates/touches `state.db-wal/-shm` (measured: `-wal` 0 B, mtime = open time, both moved during boot 2's build) — so within one process the second key always differs (05:33 pair), and `never_converged` cannot see it because it needs four write-backs and there are one or two. Certain within-process; between-boot contribution depends on whether the previous serve closed its connections (two readings).
3. **The closure depends on process-global mutable state** (`os.environ["HERMES_HOME"]` exported by `persona_profile_context` during the build's own `agents_readiness`/`prompt_observability`) — one measured divergence (`inputs=24344`). Sporadic; makes both consult and write-back non-deterministic under concurrency.
4. **The read-miss line carries no diff and the sidecar keeps no entries** — every one of the above was invisible in the field and had to be reconstructed here; the operator sees `reason=fingerprint_mismatch` and nothing actionable (A1-b; ledger A2's receipt cannot fire on the boot shape).
5. **forceFresh after every accepted write** (launcher `mission_control_actions.dart:37-41`) — one full core build + full decode per gesture, which is (a) most of the "snapshot build" cost the operator feels after boot, (b) the source of every `stale_delta/heartbeat` burst, and (c) redundant with the stream lane the launcher already holds, post-Stage-12.
6. **EG-3.1's stale-first is one-shot per process but there are two boot consumers**, and the office lane (which discards it) usually wins; when the launcher does win, the stale frame downgrades its watchdog to the 30 s liveness budget while the runtime is still building silently → boot lane torn down (`liveness_deadline`), a redundant hub-side rebuild (EG-3.3's fact) and three cores for one paint.
7. **The stream lane is chosen before the greeting** (`ops_unadvertised` misattribution) → argv on every cold boot, hub only after failure, EG-4.3 window unreadable.
8. **Per-rider consult recomputes read+digest+walk** (4–5 walks per boot, on the hit path too) — the "~2 s" is per rider and on the critical path.
9. **B1's orphan is a lazy `ensure_surface` on an unresolvable workspace id** — the leak minted a surface no verb ever authorised; the parity warning cannot say "never recorded".
10. Launcher-side drops/fences are billed nowhere visible.
